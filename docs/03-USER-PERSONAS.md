**Status**: Not started — placeholder

## Purpose

This document translates the "Enthusiastic Amateur Host" design anchor and the broader target audience (both defined in the Product Bible and agreed in conversation) into a small set of concrete, named personas — for hosts and, separately, for players — that can be used to sanity-check design, copy, and prioritisation decisions during reviews.

## Proposed Table of Contents

1. How Personas Are Used in This Product (a short framework — personas exist to be argued with during reviews, not to be decorative)
2. Primary Host Persona — detailed
3. Secondary Host Personas (e.g. a society organiser, a recurring bar-quiz-style host) — lighter detail
4. Player Persona(s)
5. Anti-Personas — who the product is deliberately not designing for, and why
6. Persona-Based Scenario Checklist — a short list of "would this work for [persona]?" questions to run during design reviews

## Questions That Must Be Answered Before This Document Can Be Completed

- How many personas are useful before this becomes busywork — one detailed host persona versus three or four archetypes?
- Should there be an explicit, separate Player persona, given players have almost no setup burden and a much thinner relationship with the product than hosts?
- Should an "anti-persona" (e.g. an IT administrator expecting enterprise-grade controls, or a professional quizmaster expecting a full content platform) be documented explicitly, to keep scope decisions honest over time?
- Should secondary personas (bar quizmaster, society organiser) be written now, or deferred until after the primary persona and core flows are validated?
